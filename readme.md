# Policy Text Processing Pipeline

## Prerequisites
- Ensure `config.py` and `utils.py` are correctly placed and fully implemented.
- Download the Sentence‑BERT model `paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` and place it in the `models/` folder.

---

## 0_structuring_text.py
Convert raw policy documents into structured JSON files.  
**Prerequisite**: The folders `data\match` and `data\query` must exist and contain the original texts.  
If the folders `data\structured_match` and `data\structured_query` already contain the structured data, this step can be skipped.  

---

## 1_sim_match_and_degree_quantized.py
Perform similarity matching and relative attitude quantification, corresponding to the **vertical diffusion process**.  
This script matches local policy documents against central documents sentence by sentence.  
**Running time**: Several hours (may take up to ~10–12 hours).  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\sentences\`.  
**Prerequisite**: Policy documents must have been structured into JSON files before running this script.

---

## 2_summary_of_paragraph_and_article.py
Aggregate statistics from the output of `1_sim_match_and_degree_quantized.py`.  
Computes average values at the paragraph and article levels.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\summary\`.

---

## 2_summary_by_threshold.py
Average the results of `1_sim_match_and_degree_quantized.py` based on thresholds.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\threshold\`.

---

## 3_generate_network_metrics.py
Corresponds to the **horizontal diffusion process**.  
Computes similarity and relative attitude matrices.  
Time‑series pruning is applied, but no thresholding is performed.  
**Running time**: Very long (may take 2–3 days).  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\metrics\`.

---

## 4_generate_network.py
Also part of the **horizontal diffusion process**.  
Takes the matrices produced by `3_generate_network_metrics.py`, applies thresholding, and assigns edge weights to obtain the final policy network adjacency matrix.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\network\`.

---

## 5_edge_distribution.py
Computes edge distribution statistics for the network generated by `4_generate_network.py`.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\distributions\`.

---

## 6_module_analysis.py
Analyzes network cohesion characteristics for the network generated by `4_generate_network.py`.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\key_metrics_analysis\`.

---

## 7_node_analysis.py
Collects node‑level information for the network generated by `4_generate_network.py`.  
**Output**: Results are saved in `results\metrics\`.
